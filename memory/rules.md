## Redefining "Quests" (Not n8n Workflows)

**n8n is a Tool:** It is the machinery, the hammer, the spellbook. It is not the Quest.

**A "Quest" is an Agent Action:** A Quest represents the intent and the collaboration of the Party.

**Example:** When the Bard writes the morning newsletter, that is a Quest. The Bard may use an n8n webhook to fetch data, but the n8n execution is just a sub-task.

**The Chronicles Log:** This must reflect Agent Actions, not just webhook pings.

**Example Entry:**
> The Bard consulted The Sage's valuation data and completed the [Morning Report] Quest.
